- S2 (issue #2 "Tests leave mktemp scratch behind", body verbatim; no comments):
  > Many `tests/*.sh` create scratch with a bare `mktemp -d` and no `EXIT` trap. On macOS, `mktemp -d` without a template ignores `TMPDIR` and writes under `/var/folders`, and without a trap the directory stays when a test fails or is interrupted.
  > 
  > Observed on main 9801950:
  > - No template: antigravity-plugin-check, antigravity-pretooluse-check, codex-launch-check, codex-plugin-check, codex-pretooluse-check, codex-runtime-check, context-check, continuity-check, full-delegation-check, init-check, install-check, mode-check, orca-plugin-typecheck, parent-guard-check, profile-check, profile-commands-check, surface-check.
  > - No `EXIT` trap: codex-launch-check, codex-plugin-check, codex-pretooluse-check, context-check, full-delegation-check, init-check, mode-check, parent-guard-check, profile-check, profile-commands-check, surface-check.
  > 
  > `tests/pane-check.sh` already uses the fixed pattern: `mktemp -d "${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/tmp.XXXXXXXXXX"` plus `trap 'rm -rf -- "$T"' EXIT`.
